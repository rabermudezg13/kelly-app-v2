Onboarding Tracker Help

Excel, photos and generated PDFs remain in your browser. For Drug Screening name recognition only, a cropped handwritten name line is sent to Kelly App for temporary Python processing in memory, without saving images or names. No document is sent to external OCR providers. Imported data stays available while switching dashboard tabs, but is cleared when you reload or leave the dashboard.

Tracker

  1. Choose or drop an Excel workbook (.xlsx or .xls). Columns are matched from headers such as Applicant Name, Talent Email, Talent Phone, KSN ID and onboarding module names.
  2. The dashboard automatically reads only your assigned worksheet: Rodrigo → Rodrigo, Miccael Val → Miccael, Jorge Silva → Jorge, Demetrius Lee → Demetrius. Other worksheets are ignored. Missing or ambiguous matches stop import; no first-sheet fallback is used. Open this tool from your recruiter dashboard. CSV files are not supported because they have no named recruiter worksheets.
  3. Select a summary card or module to inspect its records. Counts reflect the spreadsheet's values; they do not update Kelly App attendance.
  4. Use Copy Emails, Copy KSN IDs or Download Phones CSV for the current list.
  5. Open Templates for a module, review your sender details, then copy an email or SMS draft. Nothing is sent automatically.

Only columns detected in your file are evaluated. Missing columns are not evidence that a candidate completed those requirements. The original rules require Pass for MDPS Training and Approved for FP Screening; other modules use their original pending/incomplete rules.

Drug Screen

Enter representative, branch, lot/expiration, time and date. Download the filled original PDF or use Print after generation. Verify the fields before use. Representative and branch preferences are stored only in this browser for the current recruiter.

Scanner

Select Drug Screening, Education Proof (and subtype), or Release of Personal Information. Choose photos and remove unwanted pages. Drug Screening automatically locates Kelly Candidate Name on the first photo and suggests a handwritten name. Compare the crop, correct the editable name and check the confirmation before downloading. If recognition is unavailable, enter the name manually. Drug Screening files use Name Drug Screening.pdf. Other document types keep their existing manual naming. Generate a PDF with one page per photo. Take Photo uses a device camera where supported; on desktop it may open a file picker. Image formats must be supported by your browser.

Checklist

Review the recruiter and date, check the applicable onboarding items, then download or print the checklist PDF.

Sender preferences

Click Sender in the header to edit name, email, office and document-receiving email. Kelly App prefills the current recruiter's profile. Saved preferences are local to this browser and recruiter; they do not change your Kelly account.

Files and printing

If clipboard access is unavailable, check browser permissions. Downloads go to your browser's download folder. Print may require allowing its PDF window. Use the latest Chrome or Edge and review generated files before sharing them.
